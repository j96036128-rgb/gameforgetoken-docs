# Design Notes: Capital and Coordination in GameForge

## Purpose of This Document

This document exists to make explicit, permanent design constraints around how coordination and capital interact in the GameForge ecosystem.

These constraints are intentional. They are not subject to optimization for growth, profit, or convenience. They define what GameForge is and is not.

If future decisions conflict with these constraints, the constraints take precedence.

---

## Core Principle

Coordination and capital must remain structurally separated.

Any system that allows capital to influence attribution, access, or coordination outcomes is considered a failure by design.

---

## What GFT Is

- A non-transferable coordination credential
- Earned only through verified contribution
- Used for attribution, access, and prioritization
- Exists solely to coordinate human effort

---

## What GFT Is Not

- Not money
- Not an investment
- Not a governance token over capital
- Not transferable
- Not purchasable
- Not convertible into any financial asset

GFT is designed to never have a market.

---

## Capital in the GameForge Ecosystem

Capital is operational fuel. It supports work. It does not define status.

### Capital may be used for:

- Infrastructure
- Operations
- Tooling
- Public Build Requests

### Capital may NOT:

- Buy GFT
- Accelerate review or prioritization
- Influence CRG decisions
- Grant governance power
- Convert into reputation or credentials

Capital flows into work. Recognition flows into GFT. There is no loop between them.

---

## Capacity and Queue Rules

- Money does not buy faster review
- All Build Requests enter the same queue
- CRG review order is not influenced by payment
- Paid requests do not preempt unpaid ones

If capacity is constrained, the constraint applies equally to all participants.

---

## Sponsorship and Visibility

- Sponsors do not influence evaluation
- Sponsorship does not imply endorsement
- Sponsor visibility, if any, is disclosed separately and does not affect attribution

Contributors are evaluated on their work, not on who funded it.

---

## Known Limitations

These are acknowledged risks, not ignored ones:

- **Off-chain labor funding**: Capital can pay contributors externally to accumulate GFT on behalf of funders. This is a limitation of all reputation systems and is not fully preventable.

- **CRG scaling**: The Core Review Group is human and must scale carefully. Evaluation quality depends on CRG capacity and calibration.

- **Soft reputational upside**: Being associated with GameForge may carry social or professional benefits. These are unavoidable consequences of visibility and cannot be structurally eliminated.

Acknowledging these limitations is preferable to ignoring them.

---

## Non-Goals

The following are explicitly not goals of GameForge:

- Price discovery
- Yield generation
- Liquidity
- Speculation
- Financial upside

If any of these emerge, they are incidental, not designed.

---

## Permanence of These Constraints

These constraints are not temporary scaffolding. They are the foundation.

If these constraints are ever violated—if capital is allowed to purchase GFT, influence evaluation, or grant governance power—the project should be considered to have failed its core mission.

The purpose of GameForge is to prove that coordination can exist without financialization. If that proof fails, nothing else matters.

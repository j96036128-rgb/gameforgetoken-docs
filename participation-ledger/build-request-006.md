# Build Request #006 — The Coordination Codex

**Status:** Open
**Type:** Multi-Contributor Creative Artifact
**Date Opened:** 2026-01-16
**Builder:** GameForge Core (Coordination Research)

---

## Summary

The Coordination Codex is a sequentially-constructed document designed to make coordination visible. Each section is written by a different contributor who sees only the previous section and cannot communicate with other contributors.

The artifact demonstrates emergent structure from independent action under constraint.

---

## Purpose

This Build Request validates that GameForge can coordinate:

1. Multiple contributors with different roles
2. Sequential dependencies (each contribution depends on the previous)
3. Constraint-based work (contributors operate under information asymmetry)
4. A non-trivial creative output that requires coordination to exist

The Codex is not a product. It is a coordination research artifact.

---

## How It Works

### Phase 1: Seed (1 contributor)

One contributor writes the opening section (300–500 words). This section establishes a premise, question, or scenario that can be extended in multiple directions.

The Seed Author does not know what will come next.

### Phase 2: Chain (8 contributors, sequential)

Eight contributors extend the document in sequence. Each contributor:

- Receives ONLY the immediately previous section
- Writes 300–500 words extending the document
- Cannot communicate with other contributors
- Cannot see the full document until completion

Contributors are assigned slots (Chain-1 through Chain-8) and notified when their turn begins.

### Phase 3: Analysis (1 contributor)

After all chain sections are complete, one contributor writes a 500-word analysis identifying:

- Where coherence emerged
- Where the document forked or shifted
- What the whole contains that no individual contributor intended

### Phase 4: Mapping (1 contributor)

One contributor produces a visual or diagrammatic representation of the document's structure: thematic threads, handoff points, forks, convergences.

### Phase 5: Compilation (1 contributor)

One contributor assembles all sections into final form and writes a 200-word preface explaining the rules under which the Codex was made. No editorial changes to contributed sections.

**Total: 12 contributors**

---

## Acceptance Criteria by Role

### Seed Author
- 300–500 words
- Establishes interpretable premise
- Does not prescribe subsequent direction
- Leaves genuine room for extension

### Chain Contributor
- 300–500 words
- Visibly connects to previous section
- Adds substantive new content
- Does not merely summarize or repeat

### Divergence Analyst
- ~500 words
- References specific sections
- Identifies emergent patterns
- Avoids vague praise or generic observations

### Structure Mapper
- Visual or diagrammatic output
- Accurate to text
- Shows relationships, not just sequence
- Reveals non-obvious structure

### Codex Compiler
- All sections included verbatim
- Consistent formatting
- 200-word factual preface
- No editorial changes to content

## Submission Instructions

### For Seed Author and Chain Contributors

When your turn is confirmed:

1. You will receive the previous section via GitHub Issue comment
2. Write your section (300–500 words)
3. Submit via GitHub Issue comment on your assigned thread
4. Do NOT share your section elsewhere until the Codex is complete

### For Analyst, Mapper, and Compiler

When Phase 2 is complete:

1. You will receive the full chain document
2. Complete your role according to acceptance criteria
3. Submit via GitHub Issue or PR as appropriate
